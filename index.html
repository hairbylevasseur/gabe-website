About

Precision craft,
personal touch.

Gabriel is a hairstylist specializing in both women's and men's cutting and coloring. Born and raised in Ventura, California, he began his career as a barber, where he developed a strong foundation in precision cutting.

As his craft evolved, Gabriel expanded into women's hairstyling, discovering a deeper passion for the creativity, artistry, and technical skill it allows him to express. Today, he works at KINHOUSE in SoHo, NYC.

His philosophy: hair should move with you, not against you. Every session starts with a real conversation about your lifestyle, your texture, how much time you want to spend in the mirror each morning.
